2  Getting to Know Your Tracker

What it does

  • Reports the vehicle's position to AeroTrack every few seconds while moving.
  • Answers SMS commands — locate, cut engine, restore engine, read settings.
  • Detects ACC ignition, vibration, power-cut and geo-fence events.
  • Built-in battery keeps reporting for a short time if vehicle power is cut.

Indicator lights

LightStateMeaning
Blue (power)OnTracker is powered.
Green (GSM)Slow flashRegistered on mobile network — good.
Green (GSM)Fast flash / offNo SIM, no signal, or no data credit.
Yellow (GPS)Slow flashGPS locked — has a position fix.
Yellow (GPS)Off / fast flashNo GPS fix — move into the open.
Tip: the tracker only gets a GPS fix with a view of the sky. When you first power it up, take it outside or drive for 5–10 minutes.

3  SIM Card & Wiring Installation

Before you start

  • Use a normal SIM (2G-capable) with SMS and data enabled and credit loaded.
  • Insert the SIM in a phone first: disable the PIN code and confirm it can send/receive SMS.
  • Slide the SIM into the tracker, chip facing the board, then fit the cover.

Wiring the 4-wire harness

WireConnect toPurpose
RedVehicle battery + (9–90 V DC)Main power
BlackVehicle chassis ground (−)Ground
YellowACC ignition lineDetects engine on/off
OrangeRelay (fuel/pump line)Remote engine cut-off (optional)
Warning: installation involves the vehicle electrical system. If you are not confident, use a qualified auto-electrician. Never cut the fuel line relay while the vehicle is moving — send the command only when it is safely stopped.

Mounting

  • Hide it under the dash or behind panels — but keep the top (antenna side) facing up, away from thick metal, for a good GPS signal.
  • Fix with cable ties or foam tape so it cannot shake loose.

4  First-Time Setup by SMS

With the tracker powered on, send these SMS messages from any phone to the tracker's SIM number, one at a time. Wait for the "OK" reply after each step before sending the next.

#Send this SMSWhat it does
18030000 <apn> Sets the mobile data APN.
Econet → econet.net · NetOne → netone · Telecel → isp.telecel.co.zw
28040000 149.248.215.167 5013 Points the tracker at the AeroTrack server and port.
37100000 Enables GPRS mode — positions go over the internet, not SMS.
48960000E00 Sets the clock to UTC (the app converts to local time).
5RCONF Reads the settings back — confirm it shows 149.248.215.167 and 5013.
Note: on some firmware revisions each command must end with the password, e.g. 8040000 149.248.215.167 5013 0000. If a command replies "ERROR" or nothing, try the same command with 0000 appended.
Go live: after step 5, drive outside for a few minutes. The tracker appears on your AeroTrack map within about 5 minutes.

5  Using AeroTrack (Everyday Operation)

See your vehicle

  • Open the AeroTrack app and sign in — nothing to install, works on phone or computer.
  • Tap a vehicle: the map shows its last position, speed and heading.
  • Last seen tells you when it last reported. Old times mean the tracker is off, out of signal, or out of data credit.

Send a command

  1. Open the Commands page.
  2. Choose the vehicle and the command — e.g. engine cut, resume oil, reboot.
  3. Press send. Watch the status column:
StatusMeaning
pendingQueued — waiting for the tracker to connect or answer.
sentDelivered to the tracker — awaiting its confirmation.
ackedThe tracker confirmed it received the command. Done.
failedDelivery failed — check the tracker is online and powered.
Engine cut-off is a safety feature. Send it only when the vehicle is stationary. AeroTrack's H02 encoder gates the cut on speed — but always verify the vehicle is stopped first.

History & alerts

  • The Replay page draws the day's route — click any point for time & speed.
  • Ignition on/off, power-cut and geo-fence events appear in Alerts.

6  SMS Command Reference

Send these from any phone to the tracker SIM. Add 0000 at the end if your firmware requires the password.

SMSFunction
RCONFRead back all current settings (APN, server, port, interval).
RESETReboot the tracker.
8030000 <apn>Set mobile data APN.
8040000 <ip> <port>Set reporting server and port.
7100000GPRS mode — report over the internet.
7000000SMS mode — stop internet reporting.
8960000E00Set timezone (E00 = UTC; E02 = UTC+2, etc.).
8050000 <seconds>Set the upload interval (e.g. 8050000 30 = every 30 s).
G1234#Reply with a Google Maps link to the current position.
DYD,000000#Cut engine / fuel (use only when stopped!).
HFYD,000000#Restore engine / fuel supply.
STATUS#Reply with battery, GPS, GSM and ACC status.
Note: commands sent from the AeroTrack Commands page travel down the tracker's live internet connection — no SMS needed and no SMS cost. SMS commands are for initial setup and recovery only.

7  Troubleshooting

SymptomLikely cause & fix
No reply to SMS at allSIM has no airtime / SMS blocked / PIN still on. Test the SIM in a phone first.
SMS works but nothing on the mapWrong server or APN — send RCONF, compare with step 4 of this manual. No data credit — top up the SIM.
Position stuck / won't updateNo GPS fix — move the vehicle into the open; check the tracker isn't shielded by metal.
Green LED flashing fastNo GSM signal or SIM not seated — re-seat SIM, check coverage.
Everything deadCheck red/black wiring and inline fuse; confirm 9–90 V supply at the connector.
Command stays "pending"Tracker offline — it delivers the moment the tracker next connects. Check its lights and SIM credit.
Map position is in the sea / far awayTracker reporting 0,0 before first fix — wait for a real GPS lock outside.
Golden rule: almost every problem is one of three things — SIM credit, GPS sky view, or a wrong APN. Check those first.

Safety notes

  • Do not install where the tracker can get wet or overheat.
  • Engine cut-off must only be wired by a qualified auto-electrician and only activated on a stationary vehicle.
  • Tracking people without their knowledge may be illegal — use only on vehicles you own or manage.

Support

Questions or a unit that won't come online? Contact your AeroTrack account manager with the tracker's IMEI (printed on the label) and the SIM number.
